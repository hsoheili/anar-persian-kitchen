What we cook, and who we cook it for

Most weeks we are feeding a birthday in Lynn Valley, an office lunch in Lower Lonsdale, and a Saturday engagement party somewhere above the highway. The food travels well because it was built to travel — rice that holds its heat, stews that taste better after an hour, grilled meats we finish on site when the timing allows.

We are a small kitchen. Reza runs the grill, his sister Mitra runs the stews and the sweets, and the same two people who greet you in the dining room load the van. Nothing is subcontracted.

  • Family gatherings

    Birthdays, Nowruz tables, baby showers, memorials. Usually 20 to 60 people, often at home, often with grandmothers who will judge the tahdig. We take that seriously.

  • Weddings and engagements

    Sofreh aghd sweets, a shirin polo course, and a kabab buffet that keeps moving. We have cooked in halls, community centres and one very memorable tent in Deep Cove.

  • Office lunches

    Drop-off platters with labelled trays, serving tongs and compostable plates. We arrive fifteen minutes before your meeting ends, not during it.

  • Weekend brunch spreads

    Kaleh pacheh is a restaurant-only affair, but adasi, halim, feta-and-walnut boards, barbari bread and cardamom tea travel beautifully for a morning open house.

Popular event menus

Three spreads we build again and again. Every one of them can be adjusted for vegetarians, allergies or a guest list that grew overnight.

The Twenty — an evening at home

Drop-off, about $26 per guest. Mast-o-khiar and kashk-e bademjan with warm barbari. Shirazi salad. Joojeh kabab and koobideh, twelve skewers of each. Saffron rice with a full tray of tahdig. Ghormeh sabzi for the table. Baklava and a thermos of cardamom tea.

The Fifty — a birthday or engagement

Buffet service, about $41 per guest. Four cold mezze including olivieh and borani-e esfenaj. Barg and koobideh from the grill, joojeh with sumac, and grilled tomatoes. Zereshk polo with barberries and saffron chicken. Baghali polo with lamb shank for the head table. Gheymeh for the vegetarians' neighbours to steal. Sweets platter: baklava, ranginak, nan-e nokhodchi.

Full service, about $68 per guest. Passed mezze during cocktails: mini kotlet, herb-and-feta rolls, spiced walnuts. Charcoal grill on site for barg, koobideh and joojeh. Shirin polo with orange peel and pistachio. Baghali polo ba mahicheh. Two stews, one meat and one vegetarian. Sabzi khordan boards down the length of every table. How it works

  • 1. Enquiry

    Email or call with your date, headcount and venue. We reply within one business day with a menu sketch and a price per guest. No obligation, no back-and-forth forms.

  • 2. Tasting

    For events of 50 or more, come to the restaurant on a weekday afternoon and taste four to six dishes. $60 for two people, credited back when you book.

  • 3. Deposit

    A 30% deposit holds the date and locks the menu. Refundable in full up to 14 days before the event; after that we have already bought the lamb.

  • 4. Final count

    Confirm your guest number and any allergies seven days out. Small increases after that are usually fine — call us and we will say yes if we can.

  • 5. Delivery and setup

    Drop-off arrives within a 30-minute window. Buffet and full service arrive 90 minutes early for setup, longer if we are lighting charcoal.

  • 6. After

    For staffed events we clear the buffet, bag the compost, and leave the kitchen the way we found it. Final invoice comes by email within two days.

Equipment we bring

Insulated hot boxes, chafing dishes with fuel, serving spoons and tongs, buffet linens, and a folding table if your venue is short one. For full service: crockery, cutlery, water glasses, tea urn and samovar, and our portable charcoal grill where fire rules allow. Tell us about the venue's loading door and elevator and we will plan around it.

Dietary needs

Every menu can be built vegetarian or vegan — our herb stews, adasi, kashk-e bademjan and baghali polo already are, or nearly are. Gluten-free is straightforward if we swap the bread and the kotlet. Nut allergies need a week's notice because our sweets kitchen works with pistachio daily; we will tell you honestly what we can and cannot guarantee.

Tell us about your event

Send a note with your date, guest count, venue address, the service style you have in mind, and any allergies or dietary needs at the table. If you have a budget per guest, say so — it helps us build something honest instead of guessing.
